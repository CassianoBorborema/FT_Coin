=== REPORT FLOW ===
Open it from the main menu: 4. Reports. Values in R$.

1 and 2. List wallets
   All wallets, sorted by ID or by holder name.
3. Current balance
   Amount of FTCoins, average cost, value of the coins at
   average cost and at market price (latest price) and the
   cash balance. At the end, you can print the balance to a
   .txt file.
4. Transaction history
   One line per operation (buy, sell, deposit or withdrawal),
   with the price of the day, the FTCoin balance, the average
   cost and the cash balance right after the operation, and
   the result of each sale.
5. Daily evolution
   One line per day, from the first operation until today, as
   in the calculation spreadsheet. Columns:
   - Bought / Sold: amounts traded on the day
   - Qty held: FTCoins in the wallet at the end of the day
   - Cost value: balance valued at average cost
   - Trade result: result of the day's sales
   - Acc. result: sum of the sale results up to the day
   - Market value: balance valued at the day's price
6. Gains and losses
   Summary of each wallet:
   - how much you bought and sold, and the operating profit
     or loss (sold - bought);
   - the value of the remaining coins, at average cost;
   - the overall result (operating + coins at average cost);
   - the result if you sold everything at the latest price;
   - the available cash balance.
   Percentages are calculated on the total bought.
