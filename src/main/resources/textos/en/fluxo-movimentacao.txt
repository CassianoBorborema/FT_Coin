=== TRANSACTION FLOW ===
Purchases and sales of FTCoins and the wallet's cash (R$).
Open it from the main menu: 2. Transactions.

Every operation is recorded with today's date, at today's
price from the Oracle (menu 3).

1. Buy virtual coins
   Enter the wallet ID. The system shows today's price and the
   cash balance, and asks for the amount of FTCoins. The
   purchase value (amount x price) is debited from the cash
   balance; without enough cash, the purchase is refused.
2. Sell virtual coins
   Same flow as the purchase. The sale is only accepted if the
   wallet has enough FTCoins, and the sale value is added to
   the cash balance.
3. Deposit reais
   Adds money to the wallet for the next purchases.
4. Withdraw reais
   Takes money out of the wallet, up to the cash balance.

Amounts and values accept decimals, with a comma or a dot
(e.g. 10.5).

How each operation affects the wallet:
- On a purchase, the average cost is recalculated: a weighted
  average between the cost of the coins you already had and
  the value of the new purchase.
- On a sale, the average cost does not change. The result of
  the sale is the amount sold times the difference between
  the price and the average cost: positive is a profit,
  negative is a loss.
- Purchases made before cash deposits existed do not change
  the cash balance.
