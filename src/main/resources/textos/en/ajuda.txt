=== HOW FT_COIN WORKS ===
FT_Coin calculates gains and losses in a virtual coin wallet.
Navigate by typing the number of the option and use 0 to go back/exit.

1. Wallet: add, find, edit and delete wallets.
   A wallet with transactions cannot be deleted.
2. Transactions: buy and sell virtual coins, deposit and
   withdraw reais. Each operation is recorded with today's
   date, at today's price from the Oracle. Purchases use the
   wallet's cash balance and sales require enough FTCoins.
3. Oracle: check the price for today, for a date or for a
   period. Prices are generated by the Oracle and used in
   transactions and reports.
4. Reports: list wallets (by ID or holder), current balance
   (with a print option), transaction history, daily
   evolution and the gains and losses of each wallet, valued
   at average cost and at market price (latest Oracle price).
5. Help: this guide, the wallet, transaction and report
   flows, and the credits.

Dates must be entered in the format MM/dd/yyyy.
Amounts accept a comma or a dot as the decimal separator.
