=== WALLET FLOW ===
A wallet holds a holder's virtual coins at a broker.
Open it from the main menu: 1. Wallet.

Whenever an operation needs a wallet, the system shows the list
of wallets (holder and broker) and you choose by the number in
the list. Use 0 to cancel.

1. Add wallet
   Enter the holder name and the broker.
2. List wallets
   Shows all wallets, with holder and broker.
3. Edit wallet
   Choose the wallet; the system shows the current data and
   asks for the new holder and the new broker.
4. Delete wallet
   Choose the wallet and confirm with Y. Wallets that already
   have transactions cannot be deleted, so the history of
   purchases and sales is not lost.
